# Closing your account

To permanently close your account, open Settings, Privacy, and choose
Delete account. We ask for your password one more time to confirm it's
really you, then schedule the deletion.

Your data stays recoverable for 14 days in case you change your mind;
signing back in during that window cancels the request. After 14 days we
erase your profile, saved content, and history for good, and this cannot
be undone. Any active subscription is cancelled as part of closing the
account, so cancel separately first if you only want to pause billing.
