# Billing and invoices

Your subscription renews on the same calendar day each month, and the
card on file is charged automatically. You can view every past invoice
under Settings, Billing, Invoice history, and each one is available as a
downloadable PDF for your records.

To change the card we bill, open Settings, Billing, Payment method and
add the new card before removing the old one. If a charge fails we retry
it twice over the following week and email you each time, so update your
card promptly to avoid any interruption to your plan.
