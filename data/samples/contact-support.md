# Contacting support

Our support team answers email around the clock and replies within one
business day. Write to us from the Help menu, Contact support, and
include your order or account number so we can find you quickly.

Live chat is available on weekdays from 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern time, and
the in-app chat window shows the current wait. For urgent account
security issues, mark your message as urgent and we'll prioritize it
ahead of the normal queue.
