# Shipping and delivery

Orders placed before 2 PM on a weekday leave our warehouse the same day;
anything later ships the next business day. Standard delivery takes three
to five business days, and expedited delivery arrives within two.

Once your parcel is handed to the carrier, we email a tracking number so
you can follow it to your door. We currently ship within the United
States and Canada only. If tracking hasn't moved for several days, reach
out and we'll open an investigation with the carrier.
