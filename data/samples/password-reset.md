# Resetting your password

If you can't sign in, use the "Forgot password" link on the login screen.
Enter the email address on your account and we'll send a reset link that
stays valid for 30 minutes. Open the link, choose a new password, and
you'll be signed in automatically.

The reset email sometimes lands in spam or promotions, so check those
folders before requesting another link. Passwords must be at least 10
characters and can't match your last three. If the link has expired,
just request a new one from the same screen.
